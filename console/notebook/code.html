ICOR Invariants & Friction Sign Rules

Auto-saved
1

Center of Mass vs Contact Axis:

When evaluating torque about the instantaneous center of rotation (ICOR), do not add fictive torque terms. The axis is instantaneously at rest.

info Exam Trap: If evaluating about an accelerated point that is not ICOR, fictitious forces pass through COM only if referenced from COM frame!
2

Pure Rolling vs Slipping Criteria:

  • • If v_cm = ωR, friction is static (f_s ≤ μ_s N) and can act forward, backward, or be zero depending on applied force height h.
  • • If v_cm ≠ ωR, friction is kinetic (f_k = μ_k N) and strictly opposes relative slip velocity.
3

The Magic Height h = 2/5 R for Solid Sphere:

Force applied at h = 2/5 R generates zero contact friction. Pure rolling occurs naturally without ground traction.

Condition: h > 2/5 R

Friction acts backward to retard excess angular acceleration.

Condition: h < 2/5 R

Friction acts forward to supply necessary clockwise torque.
