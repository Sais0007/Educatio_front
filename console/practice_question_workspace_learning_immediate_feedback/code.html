A uniform solid sphere of mass M and radius R is placed on a rough horizontal plane. A constant horizontal force F is applied at a height h vertically above the center of mass.

If the sphere rolls purely without slipping from the instant of application, determine the height h strictly in terms of the radius R.

architecture System Free-Body Schematic
No slipping implies the instantaneous acceleration at contact point is strictly zero: acm = αR.
check_circle
Correct! Nicely deduced.Option B is correct

The key concept is pure rolling with no slip: acceleration at contact point is strictly zero (acm = αR). By setting up torque about the instantaneous axis of contact or using F · h = Icm α, we directly obtain h = 1⁄5 R.

psychology
Cognitive Focus Strategy: Pure rolling without slipping allows you to calculate torque either about the center of mass (τcm = Icmα) or about the instantaneous point of contact (τp = Ipα) to bypass calculating the friction force directly.
