## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an intellectual sanctuary for focused academic preparation. It rejects the anxiety-inducing visual clutter of conventional test-prep portals, coaching dashboards, and utilitarian enterprise exam software. Instead, it positions study as an elevated, mindful craft—merging the poise of high-end editorial publishing with the effortless precision of top-tier 2026 consumer software.

### Brand Personality & Audience
- **Target Audience:** Driven students, researchers, and candidates preparing for demanding milestones who seek cognitive clarity, deep retention, and calm execution under pressure.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Composure, profound clarity, intellectual dignity, and quiet mastery.
- **Design Philosophy:** Minimalist Editorial with Glacial Tactility. Generous whitespace gives thought room to breathe; sharp hairline borders replace heavy containerization; and glacial blue is deployed like light hitting ice—sparingly, intentionally, and strictly functional.

## Colors

The palette draws strength from crystalline light, slate anchors, and warm-cool airy canvases. Color is an instrument of discernment rather than decoration.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#f8fafc` to `#f1f5f9`):** Pristine, calm ground that eliminates screen fatigue during long study sessions.
- **Pure White (`#ffffff`):** Dedicated to elevated, floating interaction planes, focused reading modules, and active input environments.
- **Glacial Primary (`#0369a1`):** Deep glacial cerulean applied strictly to focal primary calls-to-action, definitive selection states, and keyboard focus outlines.
- **Glacial Accent (`#38bdf8` / `#7dd3fc`):** Luminous ice tones reserved for subtle progress trajectories, delicate selection rings, and micro-indicators.
- **Deep Slate Typography (`#0f172a`):** Maximum contrast reading black with an undertone of cool graphite, providing authoritative editorial legibility.
- **Muted Slate (`#64748b`):** Balanced metadata, secondary annotations, and inactive controls.
- **Academic State Semantics:**
  - *Mastered / Retained:* Soft Sage (`#10b981` at low saturation or `#059669`).
  - *In Progress / Recalling:* Gentle Amber (`#d97706`).
  - *Target for Revision:* Soft Coral (`#e11d48`).

## Typography

Typography balances reflective literary weight with high-speed computational utility. 

- **Headings (`Newsreader`):** Applied with deliberate restraint to article titles, chapter headings, and key qualitative reflections. The serif structure induces a measured reading cadence, preventing skimming when parsing nuanced concepts or complex case studies.
- **Body & Controls (`Manrope`):** Engineered for dense technical passages, multi-choice answer stems, and structural UI labels. Its geometric humanist geometry keeps long-form comprehension clear and strain-free.
- **Editorial Proportions:** Body line heights sit at a generous 1.625× to 1.7× to give complex diagnostic rationales and explanatory texts maximum breathing room.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture favors open, sequential flow over dense dashboard grids. Content expands through an orchestrated fluid column hierarchy.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content canvas of 1200px. Reading and examination viewports collapse to a focused 680px–740px single-column core, flanking annotations and tools into quiet peripheral gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem gutters; secondary panels shift into collapsable slide-overs or discrete drawer units.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1.25rem gutters. Edge padding reduces to protect vital horizontal space for complex question stems and formulas.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rely on whitespace hierarchy rather than borders to define structural relationships. Major thematic shifts receive `space-2xl` (4rem), while related components group tightly with `space-sm` (0.5rem) and `space-md` (1rem).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through flat tonal layering punctuated by ethereal, ambient refraction rather than heavy dropshadows.

- **Base Layer (Ground):** `#f8fafc` — The endless field of study.
- **Floating Work Surface:** `#ffffff` accompanied by a whisper-fine outline (`border: 1px solid #e2e8f0`) and an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow: `0 4px 24px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Focused Modals & Popovers:** Lifted with a delicate dual-stage ambient occlusion: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(3, 105, 161, 0.06), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Dividers & Structural Separators:** Soft, low-contrast hairline borders (`#e2e8f0`) used only when negative space alone cannot cleanly separate parallel streams of text.

## Shapes

The geometric visual language is governed by `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius). This produces tailored, crisp corners that feel structured, adult, and deliberate—completely avoiding both the childish softness of extreme pill shapes and the aggressive austerity of razor-sharp angles.

- **Micro Controls (Badges, Buttons, Inputs):** 0.5rem (`rounded-md`).
- **Interactive Units & Study Modals:** 1rem (`rounded-lg`).
- **Full Viewport Overlays & Sheet Panes:** 1.5rem (`rounded-xl`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0369a1` ground, pure white text, 0.5rem radius, subtle inset highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`. Hover shifts to `#0284c7` with smooth 150ms ease.
- **Secondary (Glacial Ghost):** `#ffffff` background with 1px hairline border `#e2e8f0`, deep slate text `#0f172a`. Hover applies a delicate frost tint `#f0f9ff` and border `#bae6fd`.
- **Tertiary (Subtle):** Transparent background, muted slate text `#64748b`. Hover transitions to `#0f172a` text with no container displacement.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background is crisp `#ffffff` with a clean 1px border `#e2e8f0` and 0.5rem radius.
- Padding sits at 0.75rem vertical and 1rem horizontal.
- **Focus State:** Hairline border switches to `#0369a1` reinforced with a 3px ring in `#e0f2fe`. Zero heavy shadows.

### Cards & Content Modules
- Emphasize restraint: cards are reserved solely for autonomous study assets (e.g., active question stem, timed essay module, simulated exam section).
- Background `#ffffff`, 1px solid border `#f1f5f9`, rounded-lg (1rem). 
- Do not encase standard navigation items, secondary stats, or profile menus in heavy standalone boxes.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Answer Selectors
- **Multiple Choice Stem Options:** Full-width interactive rows with 0.5rem rounded corners, transparent background, and 1px border `#e2e8f0`.
- **Selected State:** Shifts background to `#f0f9ff`, border to `#0284c7`, and prefixes option indicator with an icy accent marker.
- Radio circles and checkbox squares are 1.125rem with a 1.5px border `#cbd5e1`, filling with `#0369a1` upon check.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Compact 0.75rem label text, 0.375rem border radius, soft padding (0.25rem vertical, 0.625rem horizontal).
- **Glacial Neutral:** Background `#f1f5f9`, text `#475569`.
- **Mastery / Accuracy:** Background `#ecfdf5`, text `#065f46`.
- **Revision Required:** Background `#fff1f2`, text `#9f1239`.

### Specialized Exam Components
- **Zen Timer:** Floating inline display with mono-spaced numerals in `Manrope`, rendered in subtle `#475569`, pulsing a soft `#38bdf8` perimeter aura only when within final minutes.
- **Progress Ribbon:** Ultra-thin 3px line hugging the top edge, tracking completion percentage in continuous `#38bdf8` without segmented blocks.
