Frequently Addressed Questions

Can I enroll in single subject modules or only full-year batches?

Yes. While comprehensive year-long tracks cover the entire coordinated PCM / PCB syllabus, every student is free to enroll in modular masterclasses—such as Rotational Mechanics, Advanced Integral Calculus, or Molecular Orbital Theory—with full access to that topic's DPPs and milestone assessments.

How does the diagnostic error autopsy work?

Upon submitting any sectional or full mock exam, our telemetry engine evaluates your response time, dwell speed, and mistake typology (Careless slips, Misread stem, or Conceptual gap). Missed stems are instantly queued into your personal Mistake Notebook with an automated 3, 7, and 21-day spaced repetition review calendar.

Are free resources truly free without a subscription?

Absolutely. Our Open Study Vault hosts over 450 verified formula compendiums, authentic past papers (2014–2025), and chapter-level invariant summaries that are completely open-access with no payment information or credit card required.
